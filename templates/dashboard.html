    About This Tracker

    This dashboard monitors war risk insurance premiums for vessels transiting the Strait of Hormuz, the world's most important oil chokepoint. Approximately 21 million barrels of oil pass through daily.

    War risk premiums are additional insurance charges levied on top of standard marine hull & machinery coverage. They are expressed as a percentage of the vessel's insured value and are set by the London insurance market (Lloyd's) and other underwriters.

    Data sources: Lloyd's List, Marsh, S&P Global, Maritime Executive, industry broker reports.
